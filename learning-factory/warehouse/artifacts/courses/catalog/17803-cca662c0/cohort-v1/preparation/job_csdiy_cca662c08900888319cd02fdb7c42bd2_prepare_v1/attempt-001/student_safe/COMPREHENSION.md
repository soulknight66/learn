# Comprehension Prompts

*Provenance: course-manager-authored for the bounded kickoff unit. Validation label: learner-safe prompts, pending harness validation; not official CMU content.*

Write numbered responses in `COMPREHENSION_RESPONSES.md`. Aim for 60–140 words per response unless a small example makes a longer explanation useful.

1. What is the observational unit in this exercise, and why would treating all 16 CSV rows as 16 independent comparisons be misleading?

2. What information does the paired percent-change estimand preserve? Describe one different summary that could answer a materially different question even when computed from the same runtimes.

3. Suppose the overall median and the two family-specific medians suggest different performance stories. How should a responsible report present that pattern, and what decision would you avoid making from the overall value alone?

4. Why does input validation contribute to empirical validity rather than merely code quality? Use one required validation failure from the task as a concrete example.

5. Separate three ideas: deterministic analysis, computational reproducibility, and repeatability of the underlying benchmark measurement. Which of them can this submission demonstrate, and which requires new measurements?

6. Name one causal claim and one population-level claim that the supplied observations cannot support. For each, state what additional design or evidence would make the claim more credible.

7. A catalog record contains a link to a playlist or website. Why is that record not, by itself, evidence that the link is an official course unit, available now, or required for this kickoff?

8. If you were given two additional hours for a real rerun rather than for more analysis of this CSV, what would you change first in the measurement protocol, and what specific threat would that change address?
