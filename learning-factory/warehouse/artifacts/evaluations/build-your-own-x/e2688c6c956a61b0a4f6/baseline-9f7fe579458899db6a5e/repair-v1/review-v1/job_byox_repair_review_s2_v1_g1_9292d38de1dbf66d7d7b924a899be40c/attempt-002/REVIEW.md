## Prioritized findings

No P0, P1, or P2 findings were identified.

1. **P3 — bound QEMU inside the recipe.** `sealed/reference/Makefile` captures the emulator log but invokes QEMU without an internal timeout. The builder and this review safely bounded the enclosing `make`, but a direct documented `make all` could wait indefinitely after a boot regression. Adding a pinned timeout to the evaluator recipe would make that entry point independently bounded.

2. **P3 — clarify reuse licensing if distribution expands.** `LICENSE_BOUNDARY.md` clearly separates the CC0 catalog metadata, the linked resource with `NOASSERTION`, and independently generated material for personal educational use. That is honest provenance, but it is not a standard license grant for broader redistribution. Obtain or state an explicit license before using the generated pack outside that boundary.

Two clean builds generated byte-identical aggregate hashes for the hosted executables and AArch64 ELF.
